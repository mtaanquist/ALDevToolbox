Pipelines

The page behind the Pipelines parent in Deliver: what built, what shipped, what failed and what is waiting, before drilling into Builds or Deployments. Built on the dashboard archetype (PageDashboard): cue tiles, then "Needs attention" and "Recent activity". Desktop frames are 1440px shells scaled down; phone frames are 390px at 1:1.

Spec

Structure

  • Archetype 4 unchanged: .page-head, .section-label, .cue-grid of seven .cue links, .dash-cols with Needs attention (left) and Recent activity (right). No settings, no charts.
  • Head actions are two outline buttons, "New build pipeline" and "New deployment pipeline". The only .btn--primary is in the empty state, which replaces tiles and cards and hides the head actions so they are not offered twice.
  • The subtitle is computed from the same numbers as the tiles: builds and deployments started today, then how many wait for approval.

Tiles

  • Order: Build pipelines, Builds this week, Failed builds, Deployment pipelines, Shipping now, Failed deployments, Waiting for approval. Six to a row at desktop, so the seventh wraps; two to a row on a phone, same order.
  • Failed builds and Failed deployments take .cue--attention above zero. Waiting for approval takes the warning tone above zero, built from --warning-bg / --warning-text / --warning on the cue's own custom properties (see Components, "Warning cue"). At zero every tile is the quiet teal.

Lists

  • Needs attention: .activity--edge, newest first, .is-failed for failed builds and deployments, .is-warning for approvals, an environment being deleted and an expiring secret. Approval rows carry an outline "Review" to the deployment pipeline page. Empty: .empty-state--quiet "Nothing is waiting on you".
  • Recent activity: ten rows, builds and deployments merged, initials for a person, the agent glyph for an MCP agent, "Auto" for a build started by a push. Running rows use .is-open, failures .is-failed or .is-warning as the brief asks.
  • Every time is relative, with the exact date and time in the tooltip. No zone is written; issue #942 adds the organisation's time zone.

Facts

  • Only stored facts are shown: build status, branch, commit and starter; deployment status, "app 2 of 3", who scheduled or approved, the failed app; environment health; secret expiry. No build durations, upload bytes or Business Central update windows.
  • PipelinesBody.dc.html, prop kind (populated | half | empty | loading). ShellFrame body="pipelines-dashboard" marks the Pipelines parent link as current; Builds and Deployments stay listed under it.
